# Flipwise
A quiet study desk: recall first, lectures second. Reference: awesome-design-md's Oura DESIGN.md, borrowing breathing room and a single decisive action color, without its palette, fonts or components.

## Palette
Light: canvas #F3F6F5, surface #FFFFFF, ink #203C38, secondary #60716E, line #DCE4E1. Review panel #DEEBE5. Action #245C50 with white text.
Dark: canvas #142421, surface #1C302B, ink #E5EFEB, secondary #AFC3BC, line #375048. The review panel retains its light sage identity. Body text and action labels meet AA contrast.

## Typography
Platform system sans for familiar, readable university work. Scale: 12 metadata, 14 secondary, 16 body, 20 section, 28/38 page, 96/128 due count. Body line height 1.5; headings 1.15, tracking −0.03em. Weight 400/500/600. Numbers communicate workload, never performance pressure.

## Geometry
Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px. Mobile gutters 24px; desktop maximum 1200px, 360px review column and flexible lecture list. Radii: 4 badges, 12 buttons, 20 mobile panels, 24 desktop panels. No shadow on ordinary surfaces; modal elevation uses a dim scrim. Dividers group lectures without card clutter.

## Interaction
44px minimum controls, immediate pressed feedback, visible keyboard rings. One primary Study action; lecture rows open a study preview. Theme toggle exposes both palettes. No decorative motion or photography: the study material earns the space. Due counts and duration are demo values until scheduling exists; subject, lectures and card totals come from the imported deck. Study preview does not grade or schedule cards.
